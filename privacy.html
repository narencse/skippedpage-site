Privacy policy

Skipped Page Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Skipped Page and Map Twist YouTube channels.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool is allowed to upload videos to, and read basic information about, the owner's own YouTube channels. It uses this only to upload the owner's own videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails.

What it stores

The sign-in permission is kept on the owner's own computer and on the owner's private hosting account. The tool does not collect, sell, share or publish any personal data, and it has no users other than the owner.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Skipped Page YouTube channel (@SkippedPage).
